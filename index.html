Artist Identity

Discover MOMZ14

MOMZ14 is a new, exciting Australian-born Artist and Musician set to make his debut in Australia on April 10th, 2027 at the Regal Theatre.

"Momz14 has been inspired by all genres of music, creating bold new sound fields in electronic music by combining Classical, hip-hop, soundtrack/soundscape, and African music."

Momz14 has forged a distinct sonic architecture through years of dedicated experimentation, creating a living, breathing modular studio where hardware synthesis instruments interact dynamically to form a single, unified sound field.

Classical
